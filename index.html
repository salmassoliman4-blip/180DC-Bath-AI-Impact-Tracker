Amplifying impact, yet remaining conscious.

The branch's use of AI freed up an estimated 642 hours. This is equivalent to the time commitment of roughly 6 team members, we spent that additional time focused on conducting even deeper research, and improving the quality of our deliverables.

These 642 hours cost us roughly 28 litres of water and 1.4 kg CO₂e, which are equivalent to a short car journey. This showcases how we have remained conscious of our impact, only focusing on automating high-effort and high-value work.

Please note that due to our lack of enterprise tooling, these statistics are based off self-logged data which, despite our best attempts, may not be fully accurate.
