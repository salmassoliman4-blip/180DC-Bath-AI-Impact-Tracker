Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

What this tool is

The AI Impact Tracker is an internal tool for 180 Degrees Consulting Bath, used by branch members to self-report AI use cases, track productivity gains, and nominate prompts for the Bright Prompt Award. It is not a public product and is not intended for use outside the branch.

What we collect

  • Your name, role, and project, as entered when you log a use case or submit a nomination.
  • The text of any use case, prompt, or achievement you choose to submit.
  • Any files you voluntarily attach as evidence for an award nomination.
  • Your login username and a locally stored password, used only to identify you within the tool.

How it's stored

Your login and password are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device, not on any server, so they're never shared between devices or browsers and are removed if you clear your browser's site data.

Use cases you log and award nominations you submit are stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to the branch's exec team, so that everyone on the branch sees the same shared data regardless of device. This Sheet is not public and is not used for any purpose beyond running this tool.

Analytics

If you choose "Accept analytics" on the banner, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used, so we can improve the tool. This sets cookies and shares anonymised usage data (such as pages viewed and approximate location) with Google. It does not include what you type into the tool. If you choose "No thanks", analytics never loads, and you can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Who can see it

Use cases and nominations you submit are visible to other branch members using the tool, since this is a shared branch resource. Governance and admin views are restricted to Executive Team members.

How we use it

Self-reported data is used to estimate the branch's collective productivity uplift and environmental impact from AI use, and to run the Bright Prompt Award. It is not used for individual performance evaluation.

Your choices

You can change your password at any time from your profile. If you'd like your submitted data removed, or have any questions about this policy, contact Salma Ibrahim.

This is an internal branch tool, not a commercial web service. We don't use advertising cookies and we don't sell or share your data, other than the optional analytics described above. This policy will be reviewed as the tool evolves.
